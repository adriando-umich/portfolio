A new approval cutoff adds $7.1M of realized NPV on the 2015 loans at the same bad rate

Credit lifecycle case. The lender made money on the loans from 2013–2014 and lost money on the loans from 2015. I tested whether a scorecard cutoff can approve more applicants without more defaults.

Can a new approval cutoff increase approvals at the same bad rate?

Yes. Replace the current cutoff with a scorecard cutoff, and start with a 10% test.

  • Realized NPV against the current policy, 2015 loans: +$7.1M
  • Approval rate: 85.7% vs. 84.5% today

Fraud strategy

Python, SQL
Data: IEEE-CIS

Which policy gives the lowest total cost of fraud, false declines and reviews?

A policy that weighs all three costs together, with a cap on the share of good transactions declined.

  • Decrease in total monthly cost: $64,274 (conservative figure; range $64k to $127k)
  • False decline rate (share of good transactions declined): 2.46%, under the 3% cap

Which customers do we send the offer to, for the best return?

Send the men's email to all customers. At $0.10 per email, no targeting model beats send-to-all.
