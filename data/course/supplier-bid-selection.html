The model

Decision

Accept or reject each of the 12 bids from 7 suppliers (12 yes/no variables).

Objective

The lowest total price of the accepted bids.

Constraints

Each of the 12 items is in at least one accepted bid. Each sourcing policy adds one or more linear constraints.

  • The local-supplier policy has no cost, because the lowest-cost plan already has a bid from supplier 7.
  • A second source for Brand B medium costs $999 more (22%).
  • With a $500 minimum order for supplier 1, the $220 bid from that supplier is not available alone. The one-supplier bundle then has the lowest cost, at $4,639.
  • The minimum-order rule needs one more yes/no variable: do business with supplier 1 or not.
