The model

Decision

How many bookings to accept above capacity, for each hotel and arrival day.

Objective

Fill the rooms that cancellations leave empty, with few guests turned away.

Method

Logistic regression, a decision tree and a random forest in R, with inputs that the hotel knows when the booking is made.

  • The three models rank bookings about equally well (AUC 0.84 to 0.85). The logistic regression turns away the fewest guests, so the simple model is the one to use.
  • Bookings with no special request are cancelled 48% of the time, against 22% with one request. A request is a sign that the guest plans to come.
  • Almost all bookings with a non-refundable deposit are recorded as cancelled (99%). This is a known feature of this public data set and must be checked before the model is used in a hotel.
  • The reservation status, the assigned room and later booking changes are not model inputs: the hotel does not know them when the booking is made.
