Where to build fire stations so every region is within 12 minutes

Course project, Michigan Ross MBA. Set-covering integer program in Excel Solver, checked by full enumeration in Python. Not client or employer work.

ExcelPython
Excel modelSolver model with the cost for each response-time limitPython checkAll 64 plans, enumerated
SituationA city must give each of its six regions fire cover. It has six candidate sites for stations, each with a different cost.
ComplicationA station is useful to a region only if it gets there within 12 minutes, and three siting rules limit which sites can open together. To open all six sites costs $1.8M.
QuestionWhich sites must the city open to cover every region at the lowest cost?
AnswerTwo of six candidate sites are enough, for $700,000. One minute faster costs 64% more.

The model

Decision

Open or do not open each of the six candidate sites (six yes/no variables).

Objective

The lowest total cost to open the stations.

Constraints

Each region has at least one open station within the limit. Sites 1 and 6 are not opened together. Site 4 needs site 1. Sites 5 and 6 are opened together or not at all.

  • The three siting rules do not change the answer at 12 minutes. The plan without the rules is the same.
  • Region 5 is 12 minutes from site 3, so the plan has no spare time there. A limit of 11 minutes adds site 4 and $450,000.
  • A limit of 15 minutes lets sites 5 and 6 replace sites 1 and 3 and saves $100,000.
