Q: Can a new approval cutoff increase approvals at the same bad rate?

Yes. A scorecard cutoff approves more applicants at the same bad rate and adds $7.1M of realized NPV on the 2015 loans.

Why? Three reasons, strongest first

  1. It approves more applicants with no more defaults

    Approval rate: 85.7% vs. 84.5% today. Bad rate (default rate of approved loans): 12.5%, held equal.

  2. The gain holds under every cost assumption tested

    Realized NPV against the current policy: +$7.1M. Lowest result in the stress test: +$5.1M.

  3. A small test limits the risk of the change

    Start on 10% of applications. Stop if 12-month defaults rise 1 point.

The 2015 loans lost money under the current policy; the new cutoff recovers $7.1M

Loans from 2013–2014, current policy
+$71.7M

Source: Lending Club public loan data; analysis. The gain is measured against the current policy on the same 2015 loans.
