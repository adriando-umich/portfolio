Which of 75 ATM locations are worth their cost

Self-built case study. Capital One public location finder, 2020 Census and industry studies. Volumes and costs are assumptions. Not client or employer work.

Python
Board deckFor leadershipTechnical documentMethod, results and model code
SituationWhat the reader knowsCapital One checking customers get cash free of charge at 70,000+ ATMs. The bank owns about 1,300 of them. In the Washington area, 75 locations have an owned ATM.
ComplicationWhat makes the decision hardA partner ATM is within half a mile of 69 of the 75 locations. Each owned ATM has a cost each month, and the bank needs one rule to decide where an owned ATM is worth that cost.
QuestionThe decisionQ: At which locations is an owned ATM worth more than its cost?
AnswerThe recommendation and its effectAbove about 2,100 transactions a month, an owned ATM pays for itself. With the base assumptions, 34 of 75 locations are below that line, but only 8 can go inside the access limit. For the other 26, the lever is cost and volume. Next step: apply the rule with the bank's own volumes.

    • Transactions each monthAssumption: 2,600 at a branch, 1,200 stand-alone, scaled by residents in 2 miles

      Is the volume above break-even at each location?

      No. 34 of 75 locations are below, with the base values.

    • Value of each transaction
      • Fee from non-customers$3 a withdrawal; 20% of withdrawals (assumption)

        Does fee income pay the ATM cost alone?

        No. $1.03M a year against a cost of $1.66M. It is 52% of the value.

      • Cost avoided on customer withdrawals$0.60 for each withdrawal that moves to a partner ATM

        Is a partner ATM near for a withdrawal?

        Yes. In 0.5 miles of 69 of 75 locations. This item is 41% of the value.

      • Relationships of deposit customersThe limit: residents near a deposit ATM

        Does each location have another deposit ATM in 2 miles?

        No. 22 of 72 deposit locations have none. This item is 7% of the value.

      • Offers on the ATM screenNot known. The recommended test measures it.
    • Cost each month
      • Cash service and maintenanceThe lever: $1,800 a month (assumption)

        Does the answer hold if this cost changes?

        No. Below break-even: 11 locations at $1,200, 49 at $2,400.
