Which new tracks a music label must promote first

Course project, Michigan Ross MBA. Hit prediction models in R on 289,941 public Spotify tracks, tested on later release years. Not client or employer work.

R
R scriptModels and the business result
SituationA music label releases many tracks each year and has promotion money for a small part of them.
ComplicationAbout 1 track in 4 becomes a hit. The label must choose which tracks to promote at release, before there are streaming numbers.
QuestionCan a model find the hits at release, and what does it use?
AnswerA model picks a top 10% of new tracks in which 77% are hits, against 26% for all tracks. Genre and the record of the artist decide; the sound adds little.

A hit is a track in the top quarter of popularity among the tracks of the same release year. Models were fitted on releases from 2000 to 2021 and tested on 23,180 tracks released in 2022 and 2023.

The model

Decision

Which new tracks get a place in the promotion plan.

Objective

The highest share of hits among the tracks picked.

Method

Logistic regressions and a random forest in R on 289,941 tracks by 45,228 artists (a 25% sample of a public file).

  • In the random forest, genre and the record of the artist carry 43% of the importance. The eleven audio measures share the other 57%.
  • The popularity score favors recent tracks. With one cutoff for all years, the model learns which tracks are new. The hit label is set inside each release year to remove this.
  • The record of the artist uses earlier years only, so the model knows nothing that a label does not know at release.
  • Genre is strong in part because of how the public file was collected, by genre lists. A label must test the model on its own catalog.
