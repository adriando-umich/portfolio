Each night 100 guests ask for a cabin, one at a time. 95% are leisure guests who pay $100 on average (standard deviation $25). 5% are business guests who pay $300 on average (standard deviation $45). A guest books when the rate is at or below what the guest will pay.

The model

Decision

How many of the 10 cabins to hold back, and the full rate for those cabins.

Objective

The highest average revenue per night.

Method

Monte Carlo simulation of the guests of one night, for each pair of protection level and full rate.

  • A policy of 3 cabins at $220 earns $1,294. The best policy earns 7% more.
  • The top of the table is flat: 4 or 5 cabins at $240 or $260 are all within 1% of the best policy. An exact value is not necessary.
  • A full rate above $300 loses money quickly, because few business guests will pay it and the held cabins stay empty.
  • The result depends on the 5% share of business guests. The lodge must measure that share before it changes the policy.
