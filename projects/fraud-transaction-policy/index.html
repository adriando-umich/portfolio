Q: Which policy gives the lowest total cost of fraud, false declines and reviews?

A three-tier policy (approve, review, decline) on a fraud score cuts the total monthly cost by $64k and keeps false declines under the 3% cap.

Why? Three reasons, strongest first

  1. It stops almost twice the fraud dollars

    Share of fraud dollars stopped: 71.2% vs. 38.4% for a fixed rule set.

  2. The total cost falls, not only the fraud loss

    Total monthly cost = fraud loss that gets through + cost of false declines + cost of reviews. Decrease: $64k to $127k per month, depending on the baseline. $64k is the lowest figure.

  3. Good customers stay protected

    False decline rate (share of good transactions declined): 2.46%, under the cap of 3%.

What each tool does in this case

SQLJoins the transaction table and the identity table, with DuckDB.
PythonRuns the whole analysis in one notebook that gives the same result on each run.
Machine learningScores fraud with gradient boosting, ranks transactions by expected cost, and sets the three tiers.
StatisticsValidates over time, tracks drift with the population stability index, and tests the sensitivity to costs.
