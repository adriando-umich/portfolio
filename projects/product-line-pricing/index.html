How to reprice 15 products when price sensitivity differs by product

Raise 12 prices and cut 3. Expected profit per customer rises 31%, with each price inside a ±20% limit.

The model

Demand

Each customer chooses one of 15 products or no purchase. The price effect is different for each product, through six product features.

Decision

The 15 prices, each within 20% of the current average price.

Objective

The highest expected profit per customer: margin times the probability of a purchase, summed over the products.

  • Eight products go to the +20% limit, so the limit decides most of the answer. A test in the market must come before larger increases.
  • The gain has a cost: the share of customers who buy falls from 10.5% to 7.2%. If customer count has value beyond this purchase, the increase must be smaller.
  • Products 7, 8 and 14 have high margins and price-sensitive customers. A lower price brings more than enough volume.
  • The demand model has no competitor prices. The result assumes that competitors do not react.
