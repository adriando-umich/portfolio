One worker makes 40 units a month for $2,400. A hire costs $1,800, a layoff $1,200, a worker-month of overtime $3,300, and one unit in inventory $8 a month. The plant makes 13,000 units a month at most.

The model

Decision

For each of 12 months: hires, layoffs, workers on overtime. 36 whole-number variables.

Objective

The lowest total of salaries, hire and layoff costs, overtime and inventory cost.

Constraints

Demand is met each month from production and inventory. Production is inside plant capacity. Each worker does one overtime shift at most.

  • Salaries are 85% to 90% of the cost in each strategy. The strategies differ in how they pay for the peak: inventory ($600k in the level plan) or overtime ($412k when production follows demand).
  • The mixed plan pays $188k for inventory and $251k for overtime, plus $255k to change the workforce.
  • The whole-number plan costs only $160 more than the plan with fractions of a worker, so the rounding has no practical effect.
  • The plan uses one demand forecast. A plan for use must be tested against a high and a low forecast.
