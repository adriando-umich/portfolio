Offer targeting for an email campaign

SummaryTools

Hillstrom public email test data

R

Q: Which customers do we send the offer to, for the best return?

Send the men's email to all customers and keep a 10% holdout. It earns $13k per campaign of 100,000 customers.

Why? Three reasons, strongest first

  1. The email raises spend

    Spend effect against the no-email group: +$0.77 per customer in the 2 weeks after the email.

  2. The extra margin is larger than the contact cost

    30% margin on $0.77, less $0.10 for each email, gives $13,095 of profit per 100,000 customers. The email pays back at any cost below $0.23.

  3. A targeting model adds nothing at this cost

    Two uplift models did not beat a simple ranking by purchase history, and at $0.10 per email no segment earns more than an email to all customers.

What each tool does in this case

RRuns the whole analysis in one notebook in base R.
StatisticsDesigns and reads the experiment: power and sample size, Welch t-test, bootstrap, CUPED and the Holm correction.
Machine learningTests two uplift models (T-learner and uplift tree). They do not beat the ranking by purchase history, so the recommendation does not use them.
