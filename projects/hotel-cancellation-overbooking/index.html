How many rooms a hotel can overbook, given the cancellation risk of each booking

Course project, Michigan Ross MBA. Cancellation models in R on 119,390 public hotel bookings, tested on a later year. Not client or employer work.

R
R scriptModels and the business result
SituationTwo hotels took about 119,000 bookings in two years. 37% of the bookings were cancelled.
ComplicationA cancelled booking leaves an empty room. The hotels can overbook, but with one average cancellation rate they turn away guests on the days when few bookings cancel.
QuestionHow many bookings can a hotel accept above capacity on each day?
AnswerA model of cancellation risk recovers 12,093 cancelled bookings in the test period and turns away 95 guests. A flat-rate rule turns away 868.

Overbooking trades empty rooms against guests with no room; a better forecast improves the two at the same time

For each hotel and arrival day, the hotel overbooks by the expected number of cancellations less one standard deviation. Models were fitted on arrivals in 2015 and 2016 and tested on 40,687 bookings that arrive in 2017.

The model

Decision

How many bookings to accept above capacity, for each hotel and arrival day.

Objective

Fill the rooms that cancellations leave empty, with few guests turned away.

Method

Logistic regression, a decision tree and a random forest in R, with inputs that the hotel knows when the booking is made.

  • The three models rank bookings about equally well (AUC 0.84 to 0.85). The logistic regression turns away the fewest guests, so the simple model is the one to use.
  • Bookings with no special request are cancelled 48% of the time, against 22% with one request. A request is a sign that the guest plans to come.
  • Almost all bookings with a non-refundable deposit are recorded as cancelled (99%). This is a known feature of this public data set and must be checked before the model is used in a hotel.
  • The reservation status, the assigned room and later booking changes are not model inputs: the hotel does not know them when the booking is made.
